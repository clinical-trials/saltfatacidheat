# Fuchsia — branding notes & future decisions

A living doc for brand direction. Nothing here is locked except the current name.

## Current identity (in use)
- **Name: Fuchsia** — the correct dictionary spelling. (Common misspelling: *fuschia* — avoid it, and grab it defensively if we register a domain.)
- **The device:** the four elements of *Salt, Fat, Acid, Heat* are hidden inside the name — **f‑u‑c‑h‑s‑i‑a** carries **F**at, **H**eat, **S**alt, **A**cid. Name = method = the hero color.
- **Hero color:** fuchsia `#e01a79`. Elements are color-coded everywhere: Salt = slate, Fat = gold, Acid = green, **Heat = fuchsia** (so the brand color *is* the H).
- **Aesthetic:** cooking as chemistry — a flavor lab. Graduated test tubes, ingredient "dispensers," chemistry dots on chips, a stocked pantry, a fridge-as-lab. Signature line: **"Flavor = Salt + Fat + Acid + Heat. Balance is everything."**
- **Voice:** warm, playful, experimental, and **inclusive** — *every ingredient's invited*. Say "Sunday supper," not "family" (inclusive of who's at the table and what's in the fridge).
- **Mascot:** a cheek-stuffing hamster (nature's anti-waste animal). Secondary marks: flask / beaker glyph for the lab side.

## Open decision: "Fuchsia" vs "Fuchsia Labs"
- **Fuchsia** — clean, ownable, the name *is* the method. Best as the consumer product name.
- **Fuchsia Labs** — leans into the science-lab aesthetic we've built; reads as a studio/company umbrella (could sit above "Fuchsia" the app, and future siblings). Good if this becomes a small suite of experimental cooking tools.
- **Recommendation:** keep **Fuchsia** as the product/app name; hold **Fuchsia Labs** in reserve as the parent/company brand. Revisit if/when there's a second product or a company entity.

## Element language (canonical, from the flavor-lab reference)
Use these crisp role pairs anywhere elements are described:
- **Salt** — enhances flavor · controls moisture
- **Fat** — adds richness · carries flavor
- **Acid** — brightens flavor · balances taste
- **Heat** — transforms texture · develops flavor
